# Northwind Traders — Employee Handbook (Sample)

## 1. Vacation Policy

Full-time employees are entitled to 15 days of paid vacation per calendar
year, accrued at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Vacation time must be
requested at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal and approved
by a direct manager. Unused vacation days may be carried over to the
following year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any balance beyond that cap is
forfeited at year-end.

## 2. Sick Leave Policy

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year, available from the
first day of employment. A doctor's note is required for any absence
longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over between
years and is not paid out upon termination.

## 3. Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed
every 6 months. Remote employees must be reachable during core hours,
10am-3pm in their local timezone, and must attend in-person meetings when
requested with at least 5 business days' notice.

## 4. Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses under $500 can be self-approved and submitted through
the expense portal within 30 days of the purchase. Expenses over $500
require manager approval before the purchase is made. Travel expenses
(flights, hotels, meals) must be submitted within 30 days of the trip's
end date, with itemized receipts attached. Reimbursements are processed
within 10 business days of approval.

## 5. Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave must be taken
within 12 months of the birth or adoption date and can be split into two
blocks of at least 2 weeks each. Parental leave runs concurrently with
any legally mandated leave in the employee's jurisdiction.

## 6. Equipment and IT Policy

New employees receive a laptop and a one-time $300 home-office stipend
during onboarding. IT equipment must be returned within 14 days of
separation from the company. Lost or damaged equipment due to negligence
may be deducted from final pay where legally permitted.
